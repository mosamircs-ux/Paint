Lumina Paint Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Lumina Paint is a drawing app for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles. Lumina Paint does not require an account, and it does not upload your artwork.

Information the app handles

  • Your artwork and files. Drawings stay on your device. The app saves images only where you choose, and it sends them elsewhere only when you use the share option.
  • App settings. Brush size, colors, and canvas background are stored on your device.
  • Advertising data. The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may process device and ad data, as described below.

How we use information

  • To run the drawing features and remember your settings.
  • To show ads that help keep the app free.

We do not sell personal information.

Advertising

Lumina Paint uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show ads. Google may use the Android Advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information, and app interaction data to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings, and you can limit ad personalization in your Google ad settings.

For details, see Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from sites and apps.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: load and show ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by ads.
  • Storage (Android 9 and earlier): save your drawings to your device.

Lumina Paint does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Storage and sharing

Your artwork stays on your device. Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device. If Android backup is turned on, your device may back up app data to your Google account. When you share an image, the app you choose receives it, and that app's privacy policy applies.

Children

Lumina Paint is for all ages. Ads shown in the app are served by Google, which applies its own restrictions. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

  • Delete drawings from your device at any time.
  • Clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Limit ad personalization and reset your advertising ID in Android and Google settings.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will appear on this page, with a new "Last updated" date.
